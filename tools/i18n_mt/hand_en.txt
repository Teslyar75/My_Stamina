ONBOARD SYSTEMS SETTINGS
SYSTEMS
Interface language
Русский, українська, English, Deutsch. Only the program's labels change; typing texts, dictionaries and books stay as they are. The new language takes effect after a restart — the “RESTART” button does it right away (progress is saved).
⟳ RESTART
Key sounds
Typewriter click on a correct key, a “bzzt” on an error, a bell at the end of a mission. Hotkey F9.
Volume
Sounds are synthesized by the program and stored in stamina/sounds.
TEST
UPLINK translator
Translates the current sentence of your own text via the internet (Google / MyMemory). Cache: the cache folder next to the program.
UPLINK: translate into
The book's language is detected automatically (German, English, Russian, Ukrainian…). The translation goes into the chosen language; AUTO = interface language. Russian or Ukrainian text is translated into English. Needs internet (Google / MyMemory); offline only cached phrases are available.
AUTO
Star field
Stars in the viewport fly faster when you type faster. Turn off if the laptop gets hot.
Living space
In every section stars, comets, planets and asteroids drift behind the glass panels. “Light” (default) — fewer objects and frames, “Full” — smoother (up to 24 fps). Needs Pillow. With Windows “reduce motion” the picture is still.
